Privacy policy

What we collect

When you create an account we store your full name, e-mail address, phone number and password. When you contribute to the map we store what you submit: shelters, reviews and reports. We do not collect anything else about you — there is no advertising and no tracking.

Why we collect your e-mail and phone

We ask for both an e-mail address and a phone number because an account is only useful after you prove it is yours. We send a one-time code to each of them to verify your identity; until both are verified you can log in but not submit. The same two contacts are how you recover your account — password reset uses your e-mail, and changing either contact is confirmed with a code to the new value. We use them for these purposes only.

How we store and protect your data

  • Your e-mail address and phone number are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Lookups such as login and duplicate checks run on a separate one-way index that cannot be turned back into your contact — the encrypted values themselves are never compared.
  • Your password is stored as a one-way hash. Nobody, including us, can read it back; recovery is always by code, never by asking us.
  • The encryption keys are kept outside the database, are never written into the code or the logs.

Your location

We only ever see location when you ask for it. The “Show shelters around you” button and the “Use my location” option on the submit form first show your browser’s own permission prompt — declining leaves you exactly where you were. On the map, the nearest shelter is worked out inside your browser; your live position is never sent to our servers. If you submit a shelter at your position, only the coordinate you chose is stored, as part of that submission. We never infer your location from your IP address.

Who sees your data

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising or any other commercial purpose. The only third parties involved are the e-mail and SMS services that deliver the one-time verification and reset codes; they see the destination contact in order to send the message. Shelter data you submit becomes part of the public community list, attributed to no one once any account is deleted.

Your choices

Every right below works from the account page — no request needed:

  • Download your data — a JSON export of your profile and everything you submitted.
  • Delete your account — your declared private shelters are removed, your public submissions stay on the map without attribution, your reviews are removed, and you are logged out everywhere.
  • Change your e-mail or phone — confirmed with a one-time code to the new value.
  • Reset your password — by code to your e-mail.

How long we keep your data

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists; deleting the account removes it. We do not currently run automatic deletion of inactive accounts, and we will update this policy if that changes.

Contact and changes

There is no general inbox yet — the ways to reach us are the account flows above (the password-reset and contact-change codes are sent to your registered e-mail). We may update this policy as the app evolves; the version you read on this page is the one in force when you read it. OpenShelter is governed by the terms of use as well.
