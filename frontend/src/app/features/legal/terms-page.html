Terms of use

What OpenShelter is

OpenShelter is a community-maintained list of shelters and safe places. It is not an official emergency service. In an emergency, call 112 and rely on official information: the Rescue Board and Maa-amet. Nothing on this site is a substitute for official guidance.

Accounts and verification

You may browse the map without an account. To submit shelters or reviews you need an account, and an account becomes contributing after you verify both your e-mail address and your phone number with one-time codes. You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for the accuracy of the contacts you register.

Contributions

  • Submit only places you know to exist, with details accurate to the best of your knowledge. Deliberately false or misleading shelter data is abuse of the service.
  • To keep the list usable, the app applies limits: a daily cap on how many shelters one account may submit, a cap on how often one-time codes may be requested, and detection of near-duplicate submissions. Exceeding a limit produces an error and a suggested wait — it is not a ban.
  • Submissions enter the list as community reports. Community reports and moderator actions can hide or remove abusive content; a submission can therefore be reviewed, hidden or rejected.

Trust labels

A verified user has proved ownership of an e-mail address and a phone number. That says nothing about the accuracy of what they submit: a verified user is not a verified shelter. Shelter rows carry their own trust state (new, confirmed, or hidden), which is what the map's filters operate on.

Personal data

How your e-mail, phone, password and submitted content are collected, stored, protected and deleted is set out in the privacy policy.
